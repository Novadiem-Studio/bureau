# Workflow: message-framing

**When to use:** you're about to write something user-facing and want the framing right
from the start — choose the angle and language for the audience before drafting. Outreach,
a landing headline, an announcement, a pitch, a reply to a specific person. Also for
producing audience-specific variants of one core message.

**Type:** mixed (produces drafts)

**Inputs:** the message intent (what it needs to say and why); the audience(s); where it appears.

**Outputs:** a framed draft tuned to the audience (or one variant per audience), plus a note
on the value system targeted. Returned to you.

**Leans on skills:** `spiral-dynamics` (framing + audience variants), `humanizer` (house voice).

## Steps

1. **The Counselor** (Voice, **standard**, mode: frame) — classify the audience's value system, choose
   the framing, and draft in the house voice. For multiple audiences, produce a variant each.

The counterpart on the other end is `copy-review` (The Counselor in review mode), for checking
copy after it's written.
